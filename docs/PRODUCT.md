# Product

Clef is a desktop-first workspace for pianists who want a truthful picture of their playing. It combines repertoire management, future performance analysis, and transferable technical development in one restrained, premium interface.

## What it tracks

- Repertoire at the arrangement level, including status, clean tempo, target tempo, weak sections, and mastery.
- Individual performance attempts against the exact score version used for analysis.
- Long-term practice volume, consistency, score trends, and meaningful improvements.
- Technique Lab skills such as sight reading, rhythm, scales, chords, arpeggios, octaves, jumps, and tempo control.

## Three distinct progression systems

**Performance Score** describes one attempt and will eventually summarize note accuracy, rhythm, tempo, dynamics, and articulation.

**Mastery** describes how reliably the pianist knows one Arrangement. It is not the highest score and will eventually consider consistency, section coverage, clean tempo, weak areas, recency, and repeated success.

**Skill Rating** describes a transferable ability across pieces, developed primarily through Technique Lab work.

Phase 1 uses clearly identified mock progress data. It includes real Web MIDI connectivity but no score grading.
